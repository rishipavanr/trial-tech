📋 Instructions & Rules

🧮 Scoring

Round Score = (correct answers × points) + Speed Bonus.
• Speed Bonus: Earn up to 50% extra points for finishing fast with accurate answers.
• Partial Credit (R4): Points are awarded based on output accuracy and closeness even if not 100% solved.
• Anti-Cheat: Hardcoded print() outputs without algorithmic logic are detected and awarded 0 pts.
• No negative marking. Tie-breaks use special extra questions triggered by the organizer.

⛔ Strict Anti-Cheat — read carefully

• Switching tabs or clicking outside the window = instant DISQUALIFICATION.
• Refreshing or closing the page during a live round = no re-entry, ever.
• Each round has a strict timer — at 00:00 your answers auto-submit.
• Round 4 monitors tab switching too. Play fair. 🤝

🧠 Round 1 — Prelims

30 MCQs on Python & SQL basics · 15 min · 1–2 pts each.
Questions are shuffled per team. Once you pick an answer it locks forever — you may revisit, never change.

🏢 Round 2 — Corporate Clue

25 questions: logo ID, slogan ID, fill-ups, MCQs · 10 min overall · 2 pts each.

🖼️ Round 3 — Visual Logic

15 min.
A) Guess the Tech Word — 15 image puzzles, exact match, 3 pts each. 2 hints available, each hint cuts 1 pt.
B) Crossword — dynamic crossword puzzle. Click Across clues to type Left → Right; click Down clues to type Top → Down · 10 pts.

⚡ Round 4 — Rapid Rush

20 min total · Top 5 teams only (by key).
Step 1 (Selection): Choose any 3 (or designated number) of the 5 problem statements.
Step 2 (Coding): 15 minutes to code and test your selected statements. Partial credit awarded for close outputs. Submit to view your separate Coding Score.
Step 3 (Debugging): 5 minutes for Top 3 teams to solve the Debugging challenge (+20 pts). Final summary displays Coding + Debugging + Total.
